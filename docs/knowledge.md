## What the ASO research found

- **Brand wall, but top-10 is winnable.** Brands (Drive, Photos, Dropbox, TeraBox, MEGA) hold #1–3 on every head term; apps with only 24K–513K installs hold top-10 on "cloud storage", "cloud backup", "phone backup" (8 Sep: #1 for "cloud backup" was a 269K-install app released Dec 2024).
- **Your 11 competitors barely reach head terms.** Across 12 countries, only Fazcon reaches a "cloud storage" top-10 (#8 Nigeria, #6 Mexico); the rest show up only via long-tail phrases from their own titles.
- **Five niches:** generic cloud drive (medium saturation), privacy/encrypted cloud (low saturation), phone data backup (least saturated vs. demand, fastest ROI), free-up-space/cleaner (funded UA machines, off-niche), photo vault (adjacent intent — cover with "vault" wording, don't pivot into it).
- **New listings get boosted.** Brown Berry's "Cloud Storage : Cloud Backup", released 2 days before 15 Sep with 1 install, was already #4 on "cloud space" US. Fazcon's new ad-free "Cloud Storage: 1 TB Backup" had 1 install and 12 top-10 placements across 4 markets — but the boost fades: listings under 60 days old rank on a handful of installs, provisionally, pending velocity and retention.
- **Relevance now leads the board**: cleaner/contacts/WhatsApp terms fell to the bottom; "cloud storage backup and restore" (priority 70) topped every market on 15 Sep.
- **8→15 Sep stability:** 31 of 50 quoted 8 Sep ranks reproduced within ±1 position on 15 Sep, 2 fell out of results.
- **12-market probe** (5 head terms): 0 competitor placements in US, India, Indonesia, Brazil; most placements (4 each) in Pakistan, Nigeria, UAE, mostly via Fuzon and Fazcon.
- **Security/vault keywords are wide open.** 0 of 11 competitors hold a top-10 rank on any vault, secure or private keyword — the app's best opening since it's already named for it.

## Free 100 GB offer study

Pulled 22 Sep 2026 (AppBrain, Sensor Tower, AndroidRank, Google Ads Transparency Center, 2,908 scraped reviews). Four growth engines — the storage offer is not one of the strong three:

| Engine | Apps | Monthly downloads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paid ads | MindByte, Utility Forge, Mapidirections | 36,000 · 38,000 · 6,600 |
| Search rank | Fazcon, Fuzon | 75,000 · 29,000 |
| Old install base | Daily Utility, CloudGate | 7,000 · 6,900 |
| No engine | DataHatch, Nova, Golden, ANZ | 850 · 560 · 540 · 0 |

- **Revenue per download** (Sensor Tower, worldwide): MindByte $1.50, Fazcon $0.67, Fuzon $0.02 — MindByte earns ~75× more per download than Fuzon on similar volume. **The free-GB number is a monetisation funnel, not an acquisition channel.**
- **Offer load doesn't track downloads.** CloudGate (3 offer slots), Fuzon (3), ANZ (2) carry the heaviest free-GB graphics and pull 6,900, 29,000 and **0** downloads/mo. ANZ runs "Get 15 GB Free Storage" with 0 downloads in the last 30 days — the clearest disproof of the offer hypothesis.
- **What it means for this app** (600 installs, 22 Sep): growth engine = paid ads first, rank second. Icon = no GB badge (badge apps have the worst ratings — 3.67, 3.95, 3.9 — and 30–35% of their 1–2★ reviews are offer complaints; Play policy bars promo text on icons anyway). Screenshot 1 = backup & restore as headline, "100 GB free" as support (backup-restore terms hold 13/18 competitor top-10 ranks vs. 2 for free-storage terms). Title/description = write "100GB"/"free cloud storage"/"backup and restore" as text. Opening = target security/vault terms (0/11 competitors hold top-10 there). Only claim real numbers: reviewers report actual free tiers of ~2GB (Mapidirections), 10GB (Fuzon), 25GB (MindByte) behind "unlimited"/"1TB"/"100GB" artwork. To get a real number: a Google Ads asset test or a Play Console screenshot experiment, icon left out of both.

- **Findings:** "cloud storage" is in 45 third-party titles from 44 developers (incl. all 11 competitors) — free to use; "backup and restore" in 42 titles/38 developers; "cloud backup" 12; "cloud drive" 8; "drive backup" 5. Head terms have 9–10/10 top results from big companies. Backup-and-restore keywords are most winnable (0–2 big companies in top-10, apps with 864–27.5K installs rank). - **Rules used:** a keyword many developers carry is generic, not a brand; brand identity = a real company/product name or an app's exact full title. Tested against 551 titles: 94% are unique to one app. All 10 headlines checked against all 551 titles and a live Play search each — no exact or near-exact match.

## Competitor graphics

Captured 14 Sep 2026: 11 apps, 11 icons (512×512), 11 feature graphics (1024×500), 90 screenshots (74 portrait, 16 landscape).

- All 11 icons use a cloud; 10 sit on a blue/blue-purple field, Utility Forge alone uses cyan on white — a new blue cloud alone risks looking generic.
- Strongest references: **Fazcon** (portrait-to-landscape adaptation), **MindByte** (restrained white surfaces, attached UI callouts), **Fuzon** (expanded category sheets), **Nova Cloud** (dimensional purple/cyan materials).
- Guidance: keep the approved icon identity (four-circle cloud, centered badges); choose **one** screenshot system; build a six-screen narrative (home/value, photo-video preservation, backup progress+completion, restore, vault, scan/share); design landscape separately; treat the feature graphic as its own asset. Play limits: 512×512 PNG icon, 1024×500 JPEG/24-bit PNG feature graphic, up to 8 screenshots/device type; avoid promo wording (free offers) on the feature graphic.

## Rules for any change

- **No other companies' brand or product names** in store-listing or ad copy (Google Drive, Google Photos, Dropbox, TeraBox, MEGA, OneDrive, etc.).
- **Shared category keywords are generic and free to use**: "cloud storage", "cloud drive", "cloud backup", "backup and restore" and similar terms carried by many developers' titles are not brand identity.
- **No exact or near-exact titles** (same words/order once "and"/"&"/"app"/punctuation are ignored) — check with a script **and** a live Google Play search; never report a check as passed unless it was run.
- Competitor names are fine in research content.
- **Public repo, no secrets:** never commit API keys, tokens, passwords, `google-services.json`, AdMob unit IDs, signing keys, `.aab`/`.apk` builds, Play Console exports, or personal emails. Every page carries `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">`.
