# Cloud Storage UA Strategy

The acquisition angle for **Cloud Storage: Secure Vault** in one page: why people install a cloud storage app, which keywords a listing with no brand behind it can actually win, and the ten headlines and ten descriptions that carry that angle into Google Ads.

10 headlines · ≤30 chars · 10 descriptions · ≤90 chars · 8 keywords scored · Title 27/30

The angle

People reach for a cloud storage app at two moments: **the phone is out of space**, or **they are moving to a new phone**. The **100 GB free storage** hook is what converts that moment — it is the trigger, not the argument. Every headline and description below either names the moment or names the hook.

### How this version was built

1. **Scored the board** — eight keywords by relevance, demand and entry bar.
2. **Picked the winnable ones** — backup, restore and vault terms over head terms.
3. **Wrote the assets** — ten headlines and ten descriptions inside the Google Ads limits.
4. **Re-ran it web-scraped** — then compared the two drafts line by line.

01 · Executive summary

## What this strategy bets on

A listing with no brand behind it will not take a head term off the big companies. It can own the phrases they never bothered to write into their titles.

Driving force

### Low storage, or a new phone

Installs come from a moment of need, not from browsing the category. The 100 GB free storage hook is the conversion trigger sitting on top of that moment.

Niche core terms

### Cloud storage · cloud backup · cloud drive

The category head terms. High demand, brand-held top three — worth carrying in the copy for relevance, not worth treating as the whole plan.

Winnable terms

### Secure vault · backup and restore · photo backup

Low competition and directly on-intent. This is where a new listing can take a top-10 slot, and where the app is already named for the job.

Policy

### Generic, not brand

*Vault*, *cloud drive* and *cloud storage* are category terms carried across many competitor titles, so they are free to use. No other company's product name appears anywhere in this copy.

### Title strategy

| Title | Length | Standing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cloud Storage: Secure Vault** | 27/30 | Selected — pairs the head term with the open "secure vault" niche. |
| Cloud Storage & Backup Drive | 28/30 | Alternative — the widest keyword coverage of the three. |
| Cloud Drive: Secure File Vault | 28/30 | Alternative — leans hardest into the vault niche. |

"Cloud Storage: Secure Vault" is the title live on Google Play today. The two alternatives are held for a future listing test, not queued to ship.

## What was verified, and what still has to be

The rule in this repository is that a check is never reported as passed unless it was run. Here is where each one stands for this version.

Run · 23 Sep 2026

**Character counts.** All twenty assets re-counted with a script. Every one is inside the Google Ads limit. The source draft mislabelled headline 3 and all ten descriptions by one to five characters; the figures on this page are the counted ones.

Run · 23 Sep 2026

**Brand names.** No other company's brand or product name appears in any headline or description. "Cloud storage", "cloud drive", "cloud backup" and "backup and restore" are category terms carried by many developers' titles and are free to use.

Not yet run

**Title-collision check.** This set differs from the 21 Sep asset set, so that set's clearance does not carry over. Each headline still needs the script check against the tracked titles *and* a live Google Play search before any of it goes into an ad.

Open question

**The 100 GB claim.** Six of the twenty assets promise 100 GB free. Competitors advertise storage numbers their apps do not deliver, and reviewers punish it. The number in the ads has to match the free tier the app actually grants — see the [Free 100 GB Offer](../../tabs/05-free-100-gb-offer) tab.
