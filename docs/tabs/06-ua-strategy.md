# Cloud Storage UA Strategy

The acquisition angle for **Cloud Storage: Secure Vault** in one page: why people install a cloud storage app, which keywords a listing with no brand behind it can actually win, and the ten headlines and ten descriptions that carry that angle into Google Ads.

10 headlines · ≤30 chars · 10 descriptions · ≤90 chars · 8 keywords scored · 15 countries scored · Title 27/30

The angle

People reach for a cloud storage app at two moments: **the phone is out of space**, or **they are moving to a new phone**. The **100 GB free storage** hook is what converts that moment — it is the trigger, not the argument. Every headline and description below either names the moment or names the hook.

### How this version was built

1. **Scored the board** — eight keywords by relevance, demand and entry bar.
2. **Picked the winnable ones** — backup, restore and vault terms over head terms.
3. **Wrote the assets** — ten headlines and ten descriptions inside the Google Ads limits.
4. **Re-ran it web-scraped** — then compared the two drafts line by line.

01 · Executive summary

## What this strategy bets on

A listing with no brand behind it will not take a head term off the big companies. It can own the phrases they never bothered to write into their titles.

Driving force

### Low storage, or a new phone

Installs come from a moment of need, not from browsing the category. The 100 GB free storage hook is the conversion trigger sitting on top of that moment.

Niche core terms

### Cloud storage · cloud backup · cloud drive

The category head terms. High demand, brand-held top three — worth carrying in the copy for relevance, not worth treating as the whole plan.

Winnable terms

### Secure vault · backup and restore · photo backup

Low competition and directly on-intent. This is where a new listing can take a top-10 slot, and where the app is already named for the job.

Policy

### Generic, not brand

*Vault*, *cloud drive* and *cloud storage* are category terms carried across many competitor titles, so they are free to use. No other company's product name appears anywhere in this copy.

The figure that would matter most for this app has no public source at all — the share of phones in each country still sold with 32 or 64GB of storage, which is the real demand signal behind every install of a cloud backup app.

### What this changes for our launch

Rank where it is cheap

**Nigeria, Pakistan, Bangladesh.** Installs under $0.30 and, in Nigeria, 68 of 94 keywords with an entry bar below 50K installs. This is where the first few thousand installs and the first top-10 slots cost what a tier-1 week costs. Treat the revenue as zero and the rank as the product.

Earn where it is open

**UAE, Spain, United Kingdom.** Gulf and Western-European revenue, one competitor apiece, and in the UAE the softest entry bar of every catalogue measured (620K installs). Spain needs a Spanish listing first; the UAE and UK do not.

Do not fight here yet

**United States, India.** The US costs $4–8 an install against a rival earning $60K a month; India is almost free but earns almost nothing and has four funded buyers in it. Both deserve an organic listing, neither deserves a budget at 600 installs.

Take the vault opening everywhere

No competitor holds a top-10 on any vault, secure or private keyword in *any* of the four measured catalogues. The one asset that travels across all fifteen countries is the security angle the app is already named for.
