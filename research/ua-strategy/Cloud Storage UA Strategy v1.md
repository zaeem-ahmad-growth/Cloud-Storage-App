## Executive Summary & Deep Analysis

- **Primary Driving Force:** Mobile users looking for cloud storage are primarily driven by low on-device storage or switching phones. The **100 GB Free Cloud Storage** hook acts as the primary conversion trigger.
- **Generic & Low Competition Strategy:**
  - **Niche Core Terms:** `Cloud Storage`, `Cloud Backup`, `Cloud Drive`.
  - **Low-Competition / Winnable Terms:** `Secure Vault`, `Backup and Restore`, `Photo Backup`.
  - **Policy-Safe Generic Terms:** Terms like *Vault*, *Cloud Drive* and *Cloud Storage* are generic category terms used widely across competitor titles without policy violations.
- **App Store Title Strategy:**
  - **Selected Title:** `Cloud Storage: Secure Vault` (27/30 chars)
  - **Alternative Options:** `Cloud Storage & Backup Drive` (28/30 chars) or `Cloud Drive: Secure File Vault` (28/30 chars)
