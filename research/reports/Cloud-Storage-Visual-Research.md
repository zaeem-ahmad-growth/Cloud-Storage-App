## Cloud Storage Competitor Visual Memory

The strongest starting references serve different purposes: Fazcon for portrait-to-landscape adaptation; MindByte's Cloud Storage Drive Backup for restrained white surfaces
and attached UI callouts; Fuzon for expanded category sheets; and Nova Cloud for dimensional purple/cyan materials. These are visual judgments, not conversion rankings.

All 11 icons use a cloud. Ten place the main symbol on a blue or blue-purple field; Utility Forge places a cyan cloud on white. Badges, arrows and capacity text supply most
differentiation. A new blue cloud alone will look familiar but may be difficult to distinguish from this group.

Fazcon has the highest displayed download bracket at 5M+, followed by Daily Utility Apps at 1M+. Fuzon and MindByte each show 500K+. The smallest brackets are 1K+ for
Golden Associate and 5K+ for ANZ and Nova. Cumulative install brackets do not show acquisition cost, current momentum, active users, retention or creative conversion.

The archive contains 11 icons, 11 feature graphics and 90 unique screenshot URLs: 74 portrait and 16 landscape. Every captured feature graphic measures 1024 x 500; every
icon measures 512 x 512. Fazcon, Fuzon, Daily Utility Apps, DataHatch and Utility Forge expose landscape screenshots in this snapshot. Missing landscape sets are recorded
as not observed, not recreated.

Sources: Google Play listings 1-11. Visual conclusions are analytical judgments; download brackets are listing observations.

## Scope and evidence

Snapshot collected on 14 September 2026 from the 11 supplied public Google Play URLs, requesting English and US listing context. User aliases and package IDs are
retained alongside current listing titles and publishers. Market figures come from the directly retrieved listing data; cached web readings sometimes displayed slightly different
rating values. Figures are a dated snapshot, not a live dashboard.

Original image responses were archived without redesign, cropping or recoloring. Dimensions and orientation were measured from saved files. Asset roles follow the listing's
icon, feature-graphic and screenshot fields, with role-specific dimensions used as a consistency check. The feature graphic was not inferred merely from a landscape aspect
ratio.

Repeated screenshot URLs across device markup were deduplicated within each app, retaining first encounter order. Numbering is an archive identifier; it is not a promise that
every device or locale displays the same carousel order. Counts describe unique published assets, not a single device's eight-image allowance. Device labels and custom
listing variants were not conclusively resolved.

All 112 images were visually reviewed through labeled contact sheets. The saved originals remain available for close examination. The report assesses composition and
messaging, not the installed apps, security implementation, service reliability or measured conversion. Exact typeface names, experiment history and the cause of download
counts cannot be established from these assets.

The library is reusable project memory: an index, per-app notes, local original assets and a machine-readable manifest containing URLs, dimensions and file hashes. It is not
an assertion of account-wide memory or ongoing monitoring. Recheck listings before a new production campaign because graphics, names, ratings and download brackets
can change.

## Cross-app design patterns

### Icon silhouette

Large filled clouds survive reduction better than thin strokes and several nested badge details. Preserve a recognizable outer shape and test internal arrows or locks at small
sizes. Nova's material depth is visually distinctive, while Daily Utility Apps shows the simplest upload construction.

Reference apps: 06, 11.
### Badge alignment

Fazcon and ANZ place their circular symbols along the lower cloud edge; MAPIDIRECTIONS places its ring inside the cloud. These create different visual balances. For the
approved four-lobe concept, keep the badge on the cloud's intended centerline and verify perceived weight as well as coordinates.

Reference apps: 02, 03, 04.
### Color system

Blue is the category norm across the sample, so it is a familiarity cue rather than a differentiator. White UI, one consistent accent and a distinctive silhouette offer clearer
ownership than adding more gradients. Nova provides a useful purple/cyan exception; MindByte provides a quieter white-surface treatment.

Reference apps: 07, 11.
### Portrait composition

Most sets place a short heading above one tall interface. Gallery images or controls occasionally extend beyond the phone. Stable heading position, phone size and
surrounding space make a sequence easier to scan than panels that repeatedly switch composition.

Reference apps: 01, 02, 07, 10.

## Cross-app design patterns continued

### Landscape adaptation

Fazcon and Fuzon redistribute phone, headline and supporting objects across the width. This is a composition change, not a crop of a portrait. The captured landscapes
usually show a portrait phone within a wide marketing canvas; they do not establish native tablet or landscape app support.

Reference apps: 02, 05.
### Connected popups

Fuzon's category chooser, Daily Utility Apps' completion dialog and MindByte's share-link control retain visible source context. This is the most useful recurring treatment for
the requested popup behavior. Enlarge the relevant element only enough to make its function readable.

Reference apps: 05, 06, 07.
### Message hierarchy

Capacity, backup, restore and media are the recurring opening hooks. Vaults, document scanning, sharing and AI organization appear later. Capacity statements vary
between assets on several listings; a future set should distinguish available allowance from paid maximum capacity.

Reference apps: 01, 04, 07.
### Consistency

DataHatch and Utility Forge publish visibly different screenshot families. These may reflect device sets or updates; no experiment history is available. A new set should
deliberately preserve its palette, typography, device proportions and callout treatment across orientations.

Reference apps: 08, 09.

## Guidance for future graphics

### Keep the approved icon identity

Use the latest approved four-circle cloud as the source. Carry forward only authorized adjustments: center circular arrow/lock badges, remove unwanted 3D frame outlines,
keep the 2D cloud silhouette consistent, match background shades within the 2D and 3D groups, and keep the area outside rounded corners white in comparison
presentations. These are recorded preferences, not changes performed by this research.

### Choose one screenshot system

For a calm productivity direction, start with MindByte's hierarchy: a short dark heading, a large real screen, a restrained blue emphasis and one connected callout. If stronger
category color is preferred, use Fazcon's consistent portrait structure. Do not mix two heading systems within one approved sequence.

### Build a six-screen narrative

A practical sequence is: core backup value and home screen; photo/video preservation; visible backup progress and completion; restore selection; private files or vault if
implemented; and document scanning or link sharing if implemented. Use actual app screens, real feature names and verified storage terms.

### Design landscape separately

For each selected portrait message, create a wide composition with the screen on one side and a short benefit on the other. Keep the same colors, source UI and
feature-associated popup. Do not rotate the phone UI or imply a tablet interface that the app does not have.

## Guidance for future graphics continued

### Treat the feature graphic as its own asset

Use one clear benefit and a restrained visual narrative at 1024 x 500. It should complement the icon rather than become an enlarged duplicate. The earlier rulebook's 1920 x
1080 size can be used for a custom landscape marketing image; it is not the Google Play feature-graphic upload size.

### Evaluate at realistic sizes

Before selecting an icon, compare the exact assets at 32, 48, 64 and 96 pixels on a white surface. These are proposed inspection sizes, not guaranteed Play Store layout
dimensions. Check arrow direction, badge alignment, cloud separation and whether thin frame lines disappear unevenly.

### Test one variable at a time

A future comparison can hold the four-lobe cloud fixed while testing 2D versus 3D materials, or hold the material fixed while testing arrows versus lock. For screenshots, hold
the message and source screen fixed while testing white versus blue framing. No uplift estimate is supported by this archive.

### Use references as design evidence

Study composition, contrast, scale and feature emphasis. Generate new artwork around the app's own UI and approved identity. Competitor storage, security, speed and AI
claims are not requirements or verified capabilities of the app being designed.

## Play Store production requirements

Google Play's current preview-asset guidance specifies a 512 x 512 PNG app icon and a 1024 x 500 JPEG or 24-bit PNG feature graphic. The feature graphic and landscape
screenshots are distinct asset roles.

Google recommends keeping feature-graphic focal content away from cutoff zones and avoiding tiny details, prominent icon duplication, device imagery and promotional
wording such as free offers. Several references use these treatments; observed competitor practice is not a template for compliance.

Google permits up to eight screenshots per supported device type. More than eight unique images in this archive therefore does not establish that a listing violates that limit.
Device-specific screenshots have additional requirements; check the intended upload slot before production.

Source 12: Google Play preview asset guidance. Requirements and recommendations are distinguished in the official guidance.

### Reference selection for the next design task

Use the local library to select an exact app, asset type and numbered screenshot. A useful brief names what to preserve from the approved app design, which compositional
behavior to borrow, the real source screen, the requested dimensions and the specific change. Open the original reference before generating a revision.

Example: Preserve the latest approved cloud icon and use MindByte screenshot 05 only as a reference for an attached sharing popup. Use the app's own sharing screen. Keep the popup connected to its source control,
retain surrounding UI, and create a portrait 1080 x 1920 marketing image.
